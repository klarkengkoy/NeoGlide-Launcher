# Privacy Policy & Data Deletion for NeoGlide Launcher

**Effective Date:** June 19, 2026

**SamiDev Studio** ("we", "us", or "our") built the NeoGlide Launcher app as a Freemium app. This SERVICE is provided by SamiDev Studio and is intended for use as is.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
NeoGlide Launcher is designed with a privacy-first approach. Most data (such as your app categories, folders, and settings) is stored **locally on your device** and is never transmitted to our servers.

However, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or your device:
*   **Google Play Services & Billing**: Used to handle Premium subscriptions and identity verification for purchases.
*   **Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics**: Used to collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to help us improve the app.

## 2. Data Deletion Request
You have the right to request the deletion of any data collected by our third-party service providers (Firebase/Google Play).

### How to request data deletion:
To initiate a data deletion request, please follow these steps:
1.  Send an email to **smyddevstudio@gmail.com**.
2.  Include "**Data Deletion Request**" in the subject line.
3.  Provide your **Device ID** or the **Email Address** associated with your Google Play account (for purchase history removal).

### What data will be deleted:
Upon receiving a valid request, we will:
*   Delete your anonymous usage identifiers from **Firebase Analytics**.
*   Remove any crash logs associated with your device in **Firebase Crashlytics**.
*   Provide instructions on how to cancel and remove purchase records from your **Google Play Account** (as purchase records are managed by Google).

### Data Retention:
*   **Local Data**: You can delete all local data (folders, layout, settings) at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app cache in System Settings.
*   **Anonymized Data**: We may retain anonymized, aggregated data that does not identify you for technical diagnostic purposes.

## 3. Permissions
NeoGlide Launcher requests specific permissions to provide its core features:
*   **Query All Packages**: Used to display and organize your installed applications.
*   **Billing**: Used to process NeoGlide Premium purchases.

## 4. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy or data handling, please contact us at **smyddevstudio@gmail.com**.
